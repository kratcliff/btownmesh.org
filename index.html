Bloomington Mesh

Locals meshing together in Bloomington, IN.

Welcome!

If you live near Bloomington Indiana and want to join a local mesh network, we would love to have you! We mostly run Meshcore firmware, but a couple of us still run Meshtastic either alongside or instead.

Join the Community

We have primarily organized on Signal, a secure and privacy-respecting messaging app. Join our . Additionally, you can find us on the Indiana Mesh Discord.

Complete the challenge to join

We use an anti-bot check before revealing the Signal invite link.

Join the Bloomington Mesh Signal group

To cut down on spam, we ask that you introduce yourself within 24 hours of joining. A simple “Hi, I’m [name], I work in [industry/profession], and I’m interested in mesh networking!” is sufficient.

If we don’t hear from you, you will be removed from the group. When joining, please use a name we can recognize, though it doesn’t have to be your full name. (Please don’t use .exd, for example!) We look forward to meeting you!

What is Meshcore?

Meshcore is an open source, off-grid, decentralized, mesh network built to run on affordable, low-power devices. You can join and be off to a great start with $50 or less. To learn more, check out our friends at OKIMesh.org!

You will need the following to get off to a good start:

  • A compatible 915MHz LoRa device
  • Proper antenna for your device (the stock antennas that come with the devices are often not ideal, so consider upgrading to a better antenna for improved range and performance)
  • A smartphone or computer with a USB A port and a Chrome-based browser to configure the device and communicate with other users

An alternative but similar mesh networking project is Meshtastic which uses similar hardware the firmware acts differently. While the area primarily uses Meshcore, Meshtastic users are also welcome. All meshers are welcome!

Help Wanted

Our community is just getting started, and everyone is welcome to contribute!

Currently, the downtown and campus area has pretty good coverage with Meshcore nodes, but the surrounding area is lacking coverage. We welcome anyone who wants to help expand the network. We especially need places to put repeaters. If you own a site that could host one, please let us know!
